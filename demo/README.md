# Demo images and rights evidence

`manifest.json` is the canonical source list: original URLs, attribution, source-page links, license statements and SHA-256 hashes. All downloaded bytes are preserved. `npm run demo:acquire` performs reproducible downloads with hash checking. `scripts/controls.js` reproduces derived controls and checks their frozen hashes; `--freeze` deliberately replaces the control hash records and should only be used when establishing a new versioned fixture set.

- `astronaut.png`: NASA portrait of Eileen Collins, distributed by scikit-image v0.25.2. The [source documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.25.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.astronaut) identifies its NASA provenance and public-domain status.
- `coffee.png`: Rachel Michetti's coffee photograph, distributed by scikit-image v0.25.2. [Source documentation](https://scikit-image.org/docs/0.25.x/api/skimage.data.html#skimage.data.coffee) identifies CC0.
- `launch-1.jpg`: NASA/Dick Clark aerial STS-135 launch view, NASA identifier 201107080002HQ. [Source and public-domain statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STS-135_Launch.jpg).
- `launch-2.jpg`: NASA/KSC, Rusty Backer/Mike Gayle; ground-based early liftoff. [Archival source and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STS-135_-_LAUNCH_-_DPLA_-_ee0d19ae4ca08fc91378cb1a653a1082.jpg), National Archives identifier 24670959.
- `launch-3.jpg`: NASA/KSC rear/underside view later in ascent. [Archival source and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STS-135_-_LAUNCH_-_DPLA_-_b10aca9d98c15f8adb49b708e9e92cb2.jpg).
- `launch-4.jpg`: NASA/KSC front/side view later in ascent. [Archival source and rights statement](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:STS-135_-_LAUNCH_-_DPLA_-_d5e6b584b63f12aee75c57a52efb3b9f.jpg).

The launch photographs are genuine separately acquired images, not transforms generated by this project. US public-domain status is recorded by their source institutions/Wikimedia records, rather than inferred from the repository's software license. Retain the credits and source links; no NASA endorsement is asserted. Public-domain status can differ by jurisdiction.

All files beginning `control-` are generated from `astronaut.png`, not real independently captured duplicates: byte copy, lossless encoding, resize, recompression, EXIF-compensated rotation, and rotation without EXIF. They are identified separately in the UI and evaluation.

`labels.json` includes all 15 pairs of the six real images and six original-to-control pairs. The implementation agent visually inspected a contact sheet and enlarged launch previews; this is manual visual labeling by an agent, not human ground truth. A positive means "worth comparison for redundancy", not permission to delete. The different viewpoints are legitimate distinct photos. No algorithm score was used to assign the frozen labels or adjust the threshold.
