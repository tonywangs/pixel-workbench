Canvas & color policy

Frames are oriented using EXIF, converted to 8-bit sRGB RGBA, and padded at top left to the largest frame width and height. No scaling. Transparency is retained; hidden RGB under zero alpha is cleared. Original bytes are kept unchanged in portable projects.

Up to 120 occurrences, 2048 px per side, 32 million total canvas pixels, 8 MiB per source and 24 MiB of unique originals. PNG and JPEG only; animated, CMYK and high-bit-depth inputs are rejected.
